Install the app on your phone

Install Transfer for your account.

If your phone supports direct installation, the install prompt will appear.

Android
  1. Open this link in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install Transfer, or open the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
iPhone
  1. Open this link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

iPhone requires this confirmation; websites cannot silently install an app from a link.

Open Transfer

Customer and Driver use the same installed Transfer app. Sign in and choose your role.
